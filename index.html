Our story

From a car shed to a church home.

The congregation at Wilson Garden developed from a Sunday School and took shape over decades through the faith and labour of many pioneers, pastors and families.

1959–1975

The Beginning

The congregation began with a small group of families, meeting first in homes and a rented car shed. A site was acquired and the early prayer hall took shape.

1976

A Church Building

After years of fundraising and shared sacrifice, the church building was completed and dedicated, marking a defining chapter in Shanthi Church’s growth.

2007

Renewal & Expansion

The church was reconstructed and expanded, including a parish hall and a larger worship space designed to accommodate around 500 people.

Today

An inheritance to retain

The legacy continues through worship, fellowship, service, Christian education and the stewardship of each new generation.
